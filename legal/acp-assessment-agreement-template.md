## 2. Purpose of the Agreement

The purpose of this agreement is the execution of an **Active Cyber Program (ACP) Assessment** in order to evaluate whether the Client operates an active cybersecurity program in accordance with the **ACP Framework**, and the subsequent certification decision.

---

## 3. Framework Version and Scope

The assessment is performed against **ACP Framework Version [x.y]**.

The assessment covers the following control domains:

• Business & security governance  
• Risk management  
• Security policies and processes  
• Identity and access management  
• Infrastructure security  
• Endpoint security  
• Network security  
• Data protection and encryption  
• Monitoring and incident response  
• Continuous improvement  
• AI security (if applicable)  

The organizational units, locations and systems in scope, as well as the applicability of the AI Security domain, are defined in writing before the start of the assessment and form part of this agreement.

---

## 4. Assessment Methodology

The ACP Assessment may include:

• interviews with relevant personnel  
• review of documentation and policies  
• technical validation of security measures  
• evaluation of monitoring and incident response processes  

The assessment is conducted based on the ACP Framework, the ACP assessment checklist and questionnaire, and the ACP scoring model (`assessment/scoring-model.md`) of the agreed framework version.

---

## 5. Obligations of the Client

The Client shall:

• provide complete, accurate and current information and evidence  
• grant the assessors the necessary access to personnel, documentation and systems  
• designate a contact person for the assessment  
• refrain from any attempt to influence the assessment result other than by providing evidence  

---

## 6. Impartiality

The Assessment Authority confirms that the assigned assessors and their organizations have not provided consulting on the design, implementation or operation of the Client's cybersecurity program within the previous 24 months, and that no other conflict of interest exists (see `docs/governance.md`).

Fees under this agreement do not depend on the assessment result or the certification decision.

---

## 7. Certification Decision

Following completion of the assessment, the Assessment Authority decides whether the Client meets the requirements for **ACP Certification** as defined in the ACP scoring model.

Possible outcomes are:

• ACP Certification Granted  
• ACP Certification Pending: the Client may remediate the identified gaps within **3 months** of the assessment report; remediation is verified in a follow-up review of the affected controls  
• ACP Certification Not Granted  

The final certification decision lies solely with **Wechsler Information Solution**.

The Client may appeal the decision in writing within **30 days** (see `docs/certification.md`).

---

## 8. Certification, Trust Label and Registry

If certification is granted, the Client receives an ACP Certificate with a certificate ID and may use the **ACP Trust Label** in accordance with `docs/trademark.md` and `docs/trust-label.md`.

The certification is valid for **24 months** from the certification date.

The Client consents to being listed in the **ACP Certification Registry** with its organization name, country, certified scope, framework version, certificate ID, validity period and certification status.

---

## 9. Obligations During the Validity Period

During the validity period, the Client shall:

• submit a written annual confirmation 12 months after the certification date  
• notify the Assessment Authority within **30 days** of significant changes affecting the certified scope  
• notify the Assessment Authority within **30 days** of significant security incidents affecting the certified scope  

---

## 10. Suspension and Withdrawal

The Assessment Authority may suspend or withdraw the certification in accordance with `docs/certification.md`, in particular if certification requirements are no longer met, required confirmations or notifications are not submitted, false information was provided, or the certification or Trust Label is misused.

During suspension the Trust Label must not be used. After withdrawal, the Client shall remove the Trust Label and all references to the certification from its materials within **30 days**. The registry is updated accordingly.

---

## 11. Confidentiality

Both parties shall treat all non-public information obtained in connection with this agreement as confidential and use it solely for the purpose of this agreement.

This obligation does not apply to information that is publicly available, that was lawfully obtained from third parties, or that must be disclosed by law or by order of an authority. The registry information listed in section 8 is not confidential.

The confidentiality obligation continues for **five years** after the end of this agreement.

---

## 12. Data Protection

Each party complies with the applicable data protection laws, in particular the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The Assessment Authority processes personal data only to the extent necessary for the assessment and certification and protects it with appropriate technical and organizational measures.

The Client provides evidence without unnecessary personal data wherever possible.

Personal data is disclosed to third parties only to assessors of authorized ACP partners involved in the engagement, who are bound by equivalent confidentiality and data protection obligations.

---

## 13. Retention of Assessment Records

The assessment report, control scores and evidence references are retained until **three years after the end of the certification validity period**, or three years after the certification decision if no certification is granted.

Copies of evidence provided by the Client are deleted or returned within **90 days** after the certification decision, unless they are required for a pending appeal.

---

## 14. Fees

Fees for the assessment are defined in a separate offer or commercial agreement.

Follow-up reviews, focused reviews and reassessments may be charged separately.

---

## 15. Limitation of Liability

The ACP assessment represents an evaluation of cybersecurity practices at a specific point in time. The assessment and certification do not guarantee the absence of vulnerabilities or security incidents.

The liability of the Assessment Authority under this agreement is limited to the fees paid by the Client under this agreement. Liability for indirect or consequential damages, including loss of profit, is excluded.

These limitations do not apply to damages caused intentionally or by gross negligence, or where liability cannot be limited under mandatory law.

---

## 16. Term and Termination

This agreement enters into force upon signature by both parties.

Either party may terminate this agreement in writing:

• at any time before the start of the assessment  
• after the start of the assessment, with 30 days' notice; services performed until termination are charged  
• with immediate effect for good cause  

Termination does not affect a certification already granted, which remains subject to the rules of the ACP certification program.

Sections 10 to 13 and 15 survive the termination of this agreement.
