# Support from Wechsler Information Solution

You can use ACP without me. The framework, the questionnaire, the self-assessment guide and the [web app](https://acp.videotrainer.ch/) (in German) are freely available. The web app requires no registration and uses no cookies, and your entries stay in your browser.

Sometimes an outside view helps. For this, I offer the following with Wechsler Information Solution:

* **Guided self-assessment:** We go through the controls together with you and your IT service provider. I ask questions where a score seems too high or too low and explain technical terms in plain language.
* **Improvement plan:** The result becomes a prioritized plan with owners and deadlines for management or the board.
* **Implementation support:** for specific topics, for example administrator accounts, multi-factor authentication, backups or the incident response plan.
* **Training:** for IT teams, employees and management.
* **Official ACP assessment:** if you want an independently verified result and the ACP Certification. See [assessment process](./assessment-process.md).

## For IT Service Providers

You may perform the self-assessment with your customers without me. If you assess your own work, the result is not independent proof, and only Wechsler Information Solution issues ACP certifications (see [FAQ](./faq.md)). On request, I introduce your team to ACP or accompany your first customer meetings.

## Consulting and Certification Are Separate

A guided self-assessment, an improvement plan, implementation support and accompanying customer meetings count as consulting for the organization concerned. The same applies to help in remediating findings of an ACP assessment.

If Wechsler Information Solution has advised an organization on its cybersecurity program within the previous 24 months, it cannot assess that organization. As I currently perform all assessments myself, an official assessment by me is only possible 24 months after the last consulting. If you are planning both, we will discuss this in the first meeting.

General training that is not tailored to your cybersecurity program is not considered consulting.

See [governance](./governance.md#impartiality).
