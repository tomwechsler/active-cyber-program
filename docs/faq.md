# General

## What is ACP?

ACP is a framework for assessing whether an organization operates an **active** cybersecurity program: not only whether security measures exist, but whether they are applied, checked and improved. It consists of 71 controls in 11 domains, a scoring model and an optional certification.

See the [framework overview](./overview.md).

## Who is ACP for?

Mainly small and medium-sized organizations that want a practical and understandable picture of their cybersecurity, for example to answer customer questionnaires, to prepare for requirements such as NIS2 or to plan their next steps.

Small organizations do not need extensive documentation. Short, clear rules are sufficient if they are applied in practice and can be shown.

## Does ACP replace ISO/IEC 27001 or NIS2?

No. ACP is smaller and more practical. Every ACP control is mapped to NIS2, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, NIST CSF 2.0 and, for AI security, the NIST AI RMF. The mapping also shows which topics ACP does not cover and how ACP relates to DORA.

An ACP Certification does not constitute NIS2 or DORA compliance or an ISO/IEC 27001 certification. ACP can be a practical first step, and its results can be reused for these frameworks.

See the [control mapping](../framework/control-mapping.md).

## Is the ACP Certification accredited?

No. The ACP Certification is issued by Wechsler Information Solution under the published rules of this framework. It is not accredited by a national accreditation body and does not replace certifications or evidence required by law or contract.

## Does an ACP Certification mean that we are secure?

It confirms that your organization operates an active cybersecurity program at the time of the assessment. It does not guarantee the absence of security incidents. That is why the certification is limited to 24 months and includes obligations during the validity period.

## Who is behind ACP?

ACP is developed and maintained by Wechsler Information Solution, Oberbuchsiten, Switzerland. Assessments are currently performed by Tom Wechsler.

See [author](./author.md) and [governance](./governance.md).

## May we use ACP free of charge?

Yes. The framework, the questionnaire, the templates and the web app may be used free of charge for internal assessments and improvement. Only the ACP Certification and the ACP Trust Label require an official assessment.

See the [license](../LICENSE) and the [trademark and certification usage policy](./trademark.md).

# Self-Assessment

## Is the web app really free, and what happens to our data?

Yes. The [ACP self-assessment web app](https://acp.videotrainer.ch/) is free and requires no registration. Your answers stay in your browser. They are not stored or transmitted to a server, and no cookies or tracking tools are used. When you close or reload the page, all entries are deleted. You can save the result as a PDF using the print function.

## How long does the self-assessment take?

Depending on your experience, about 45 to 60 minutes for all domains. Individual domains can be assessed in a few minutes.

## How meaningful is the result of a self-assessment?

The self-assessment shows where you stand and where to start. It is not verified. In an official assessment, every score must be supported by evidence, and a control without evidence is scored 0. The official result may therefore be lower if evidence is missing.

Tip: score each control as you could prove it, not as you intend it to be.

## What do we do with the result?

Plan your next steps with the [improvement plan](../templates/improvement-plan-template.md): knock-out controls first, then critical domains, then quick wins. Repeat the self-assessment after a few months to see your progress.

See also the [self-assessment guide](./self-assessment-guide.md).

# Assessment and Certification

## How does an official assessment work?

After a free preliminary call, the scope is agreed in writing. You prepare with the self-assessment and collect evidence. The assessment itself consists of document review, interviews and technical demonstrations. You receive a report with scores, strengths and improvement recommendations, followed by the certification decision.

See the [assessment and certification process](./assessment-process.md).

## Is the assessment performed remotely or on site?

Both are possible: remotely, on site or as a combination, depending on what suits your organization.

## In which languages are assessments performed?

In German or English.

## Which evidence do we need?

Anything that shows that a control works in practice, for example rules, configurations, screenshots, logs, reports or records. For each control, the scoring guidance in the [questionnaire](../assessment/assessment-questionnaire.md) lists what is typically shown.

See the [evidence requirements](../assessment/evidence-requirements.md).

## What are the requirements for certification?

* overall ACP score of at least 2.00
* every applicable critical domain at least 2.00
* no knock-out control scored 0
* all scores supported by evidence

See the [scoring model](../assessment/scoring-model.md).

## What are knock-out controls?

Six fundamental controls: multi-factor authentication (4.3), privileged accounts (4.4), patching (5.3), backups and their protection (8.4, 8.5) and incident response (9.4). If one of them is scored 0, certification is not possible, regardless of the overall score.

## What happens if the requirements are not quite met?

The decision is **ACP Certification Pending**. You have 3 months from the assessment report to close the gaps. A follow-up review is then limited to the affected controls. If the gaps are not closed within 3 months, the certification is not granted, and a new full assessment is required.

## How long is the certification valid?

24 months. During this period you submit an annual written confirmation and report significant changes and security incidents within 30 days. To renew the certification, a reassessment is performed before it expires.

See [certification](./certification.md).

## Can Wechsler Information Solution both advise and certify us?

Not within 24 months. If Wechsler Information Solution has advised your organization on its cybersecurity program within the previous 24 months, it cannot assess your organization. General training, such as public courses, is not considered consulting. This rule protects the credibility of the certification.

## What is published about our organization?

Certified organizations are listed in the public [ACP Certification Registry](../certifications/registry.md) with certificate ID, name, country, certified scope, framework version, validity and status. Consent to the listing is part of the assessment agreement. Scores and findings are not published. All information collected during the assessment is treated confidentially.

# Costs

## What does an assessment cost?

The cost depends on the effort. After a free and non-binding preliminary call, you receive a written offer before any work begins. Fees do not depend on the assessment result or the certification decision.

Prices on request: [training@videotrainer.ch](mailto:training@videotrainer.ch)

## What determines the effort?

* size of the organization and number of locations
* scope of the assessment
* number of systems and cloud services
* outsourced IT operations, as the IT service provider is involved
* use of AI services, as Domain 11 then applies
* preparation, especially how quickly evidence is available

## How can we reduce the effort?

* complete the self-assessment first
* collect the evidence for each control using the scoring guidance in the questionnaire
* name contacts, including your IT service provider
* close obvious gaps beforehand, especially in knock-out controls
